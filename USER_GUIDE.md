# Osu Church of Christ - Admin Dashboard User Guide

Welcome to the new Osu Church of Christ Admin Dashboard! This guide will help you navigate the new system, which replaces the old `checkin.osucoc.org` tool.

**New System URL:** [https://admin.osucoc.org](https://admin.osucoc.org)

---

## 1. Logging In

Since this is an invite-only system, you should have received an invitation email.

1.  **Go to** [https://admin.osucoc.org](https://admin.osucoc.org).
2.  **Login Methods:**
    *   **Email & Password:** Enter your email and the password you set up.
    *   **Google Login:** If your invited email is a Gmail address, you can simply click "Sign in with Google" for faster access.

> **Note:** If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page to reset it via email.

---

## 2. Marking Attendance

The "Mark Attendance" page is designed for quick data entry, especially on mobile devices.

1.  **Navigate:** Click on **"Mark Attendance"** in the side menu.
2.  **Select Date:**
    *   By default, today's date is selected.
    *   To mark attendance for a past date, tap the **"Attendance Date"** field and select the correct date from the calendar.
3.  **Enter Number:**
    *   Type the member's **Church Number** in the input field.
    *   Tap **"Mark Present"** (or press Enter).
4.  **Confirmation:**
    *   You will see a green "Success" message if the member is found and marked.
    *   If the number is invalid, you will see an error message.

---

## 3. Generating & Downloading Reports

You can view and download lists of who was present or absent for any specific date.

### A. View the Report
1.  **Navigate:** Click on **"Attendance Report"** in the side menu.
    *   *(Note: You can also view a quick report at the bottom of the "Mark Attendance" page).*
2.  **Select Date:** Tap the **"Report Date"** field to choose the Sunday (or event date) you want to review.
3.  **Switch Views:** Use the tabs to switch between:
    *   **Present:** Members who have been marked for that date.
    *   **Absent:** Members who have *not* been marked.

### B. Download PDF or CSV
1.  **Select Records:**
    *   Click the **"Select All"** button at the top of the table to select everyone in the list.
    *   *Or*, click the checkboxes next to specific names if you only want a partial list.
2.  **Download:**
    *   **For PDF:** Click the **"Download PDF"** button. This is best for printing or sharing on WhatsApp.
    *   **For Excel/CSV:** Click the **"Download CSV"** button. This is best if you need to do further analysis in Excel.

---

## 4. Key Differences from Old System

| Feature | Old System (`checkin.osucoc.org`) | New System (`admin.osucoc.org`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Login** | Shared password | Individual accounts (Secure) |
| **Dates** | Fixed to current date | **Editable!** You can now mark/view past dates easily. |
| **Reports** | Simple list | **Interactive Tables** with Search, Sort, and PDF Export. |
| **Mobile** | Basic | **Optimized** for phones and tablets. |

---

**Need Help?**

If you have any issues accessing the system or need to request access for a new user:

*   **For Gmail Users:** Send your Gmail address to the administrator. Once added, you can use the "Sign in with Google" button for instant access.
*   **For Non-Gmail Users:** Send your preferred email address and a temporary password to the administrator. They will create your account manually, and you can then log in using the "Member Login" tab.
*   **Invitations:** If you haven't received your invitation or it has expired, please contact the technical administrator.
